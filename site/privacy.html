What this website collects

This website does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, advertising or third-party scripts. Fonts and all other files are served from our own domain, so visiting the site does not send your information to anyone else.

The only thing stored on your device is your light or dark theme choice, kept in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) for a short period for security and to keep the site running.

The contact form does not send anything to our servers. It opens your own email app with your message filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send there. When you email us, we receive your name, email address and whatever you include in the message.

We use this only to reply to you and, if we work together, to deliver the project. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries, or taking steps to enter into a contract at your request.

We keep enquiry emails for up to 24 months if we don't go on to work together. Client records are kept for as long as the law requires for accounting and tax purposes.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information. It is stored with our email provider, who processes it on our behalf.

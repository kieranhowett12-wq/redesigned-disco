FAQ

Questions we get asked a lot

How long does a website take?

A Starter site usually takes about 2 weeks. Business sites take 4–6 weeks, depending on how quickly content and feedback come back.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. Business and Custom builds include a CMS and a short walkthrough so you can edit text, images and blog posts without touching code.

Do you handle hosting and domains?

We can set up hosting and domains for you, or deploy to your existing provider. Care plans cover hosting, updates and backups.

What do you need from me to start?

A short call, any brand assets you already have (logo, colours, photos) and a rough idea of the pages you need. We'll help with the rest.

How does payment work?

50% to book your slot and 50% at launch. Larger projects can be split into milestones.
